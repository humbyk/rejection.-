The One That Got Away

A story of love, hope, and heartbreak

They say the hardest thing about loving someone is knowing when to let them go. I learned that the hard way.

I saw her, and everything changed. She wasn't just beautiful—it was something deeper. Her eyes, her smile, the way she carried herself. Every little thing about her made my heart skip a beat. I couldn't stop thinking about her. I didn't want to.

I spent sleepless nights thinking about the perfect way to tell her. How do you put into words what someone means to you? How do you make them understand that they're not just another person—they're the person?

So I tried. I poured my heart out. I told her everything I felt, every reason why she was special to me. I was terrified, but I had to know. I had to try.

But sometimes, no matter how much you love someone, it's just not enough. She said no. And just like that, my world shifted.

"They say love is the greatest gift you can give someone. But what about when that gift isn't wanted? What about when all you have to offer isn't enough? Maybe that's the real tragedy—not that she said no, but that I'll never know if things could have been different. Maybe in another life, another time, another version of me... maybe then it would have worked. But in this one, I'm left with memories and what-ifs."

I loved her. I really did. With everything I had. She was my favorite person, my greatest inspiration. Even though she doesn't feel the same way, that doesn't change what I felt. What I still feel.

Maybe this is part of life. Maybe heartbreak is just the price we pay for being brave enough to love. And if that's the case, then at least I can say I tried. At least I know what it feels like to love someone with all your heart, even if they don't love you back.

So here's to Siba. Here's to the girl who made me feel alive, even if it was just for a moment. I loved you. I still do. And I always will.
